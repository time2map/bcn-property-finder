# SEO keyword targets — BCN property finder

Working list of keywords we target with programmatic, data-driven pages.
Sources: Ahrefs Free Keyword Generator (2026-06) + manual SERP recon.

## ⚠️ Data caveats (read first)
- The free Ahrefs tool only buckets volume as **">100" / "<100"** — directional only, not real numbers.
- KD **"Easy"** from the free tool is optimistic and does **not** capture that high-authority
  content sites (Expatica, Expat Arrivals, HousingAnywhere, relocation agencies) own the
  head-term SERPs. → Win the **long tail** first; take head terms slowly with unique-data pages.
- English "barcelona …" queries are polluted by **FC Barcelona** (football). Filter that out.
- Real volumes to be confirmed **post-launch via Google Search Console** (free, truthful).

## Language → monetization
| Lang | Audience | Volume | Monetization |
|---|---|---|---|
| es / ca | mostly **locals** | highest | low direct pay → ads / affiliate + authority/links |
| en / fr / de | **relocating foreigners** = payers | lower | paid personalized shortlist (€39) |

Build engine language-agnostic. Launch **en** (payers) + **es** (volume) first; add fr/de later.

## Cluster A — barri profiles
Pattern: `living in {barri} barcelona`, `{barri} barcelona neighborhood`, `is {barri} a good place to live`
**Validated:** seed `barcelona neighborhood` → 222 ideas incl. per-barri variants
(gracia, el born, eixample, les corts, poblenou, sant antoni, el raval, fort pienc, pedralbes…).
Seed `vivir en barcelona` → 262 ideas. → strong programmatic surface in both languages.
