# Barcelona precedents: maps and "your street / your barri" stories already published

Collected 2026-11-14 from about 15 web searches. **Not a complete list**: the newspapers' own data pieces
(El Periódico, La Vanguardia, Ara) are poorly indexed and mostly did not come up. Only the 3Cat noise piece was
opened and read; every other description comes from the headline and the search snippet, so open a piece before
quoting it.

Use: before pitching a story, check here whether it has been told, by whom, and what would be new.

## What follows from this

1. **Noise is taken.** A measured, real-time street noise map by residents was covered by three outlets in March
   2026; the Ajuntament has an address lookup; Xataka told the public-health story in 2022. Our noise is a 2022
   model. Decision 2026-11-14 (owner): noise is not the lead story.
2. **The 15-minute city is taken too, with the answer "100%".** It is usable only as a rebuttal, and only if our
   door-to-door numbers really differ.
3. **A building-age map of Barcelona is not new** (2014, with international coverage).
4. **Nobody found does a multi-criteria evaluation under the reader's own conditions.** The "best barris" genre is
   filled with lists that have no data. An empty niche can also mean no demand: to be tested.
5. **Every single-layer map here got covered** by the same few outlets: Time Out, betevé, 3Cat, Barcelona Secreta,
   Xataka Magnet. The recurring headline is "the map that tells you X about your street / your barri".
6. **The studio precedent.** Big Time BCN and the Deyde 15-minute map are both showcases by companies that sell
   map and data work. This is the same play as ours, in the same city, and the press covered them as stories.
